# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces respectful, technical,
and inclusive. Constructive disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats,
discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publication
of another person's private information are not.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, community
channels, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security-advisory channel when confidentiality is needed. Maintainers may edit
or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or ban
participants when necessary. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly
as practical.
